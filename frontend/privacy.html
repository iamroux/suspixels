Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026  ·  Operated by Roux

SusPixels is a small personal project. This policy explains what information we collect when you use the site and how it is used. There's no advertising, no tracking pixels, and no selling of data — ever.

What we collect

When you create an account we ask for:

  • Display name — can be anything you like; we don't verify it.
  • Email address — can be anything you like; we don't verify it either.

We also store the pixel placements you make on the canvas — coordinates, colour, and a timestamp — so your contributions are attributed to your account.

How we use it

  • To identify you on the canvas (your display name appears when someone inspects your pixel).
  • To send occasional emails — things like status updates, changelogs, or important notices. We don't send marketing spam. You can ask us to stop at any time.

Where it's stored

Account data (name + email) and your pixel history are stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted on Neon and cached in Redis Cloud. Both are cloud services with their own security and data-protection practices. The backend is hosted on Render.

We don't share your data with any other third parties.

Your rights

You can ask us to delete your account and all associated data at any time. Just send an email and we'll take care of it promptly.
