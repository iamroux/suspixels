SusPixels

A collaborative pixel canvas. Every pixel counts.

Overview

Suspixels is a shared 3000×3000 canvas where anyone can place a pixel. Work together, claim territory, build art — one pixel at a time.

Click to place a pixel
Click & drag to pan
Scroll / pinch to zoom
Tap a pixel to see who placed it

Rules

These rules keep the canvas fair and fun for everyone.

Prohibited
1 Inappropriate content permanent ban
Explicit, hateful, discriminatory, or illegal content is not tolerated in any form.
2 Bots & scripted insertion permanent ban
One account per person. Automated scripts, bots, or any programmatic pixel placement is strictly forbidden.
3 Griefing timeout
Deliberately destroying others' work with no creative intent. Repeated offences escalate to a permanent ban.
Fair play
1 Territorial disputes allowed
Competing for canvas space is part of the game. Fight for your territory fair and square.
2 Claiming empty space allowed
Unclaimed areas of the canvas are free for anyone to use. Go build something great.
